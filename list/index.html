How the list works


What do I have to do?
Make your card, post it on X from your own account with the text we give you, then paste the link to that post here. We check the post is real and yours, and your wallet is on the list.


Can I delete the post afterwards?
You can, but you lose your spot. We check every post again before the mint, and anything that has been deleted or hidden comes off the list. The post is the whole price of getting on it, so it has to still be there when the list closes.


Does being on the list get me a crab?
It gets you the first round. The list mints before the public, and the public round opens at 17:00 UTC. The limit is 2 crabs per wallet in total, whichever round you mint in. More wallets are on the list than there are crabs, so nobody is promised one: be there when it opens.


Why a post instead of a form?
Forms get filled by bots in minutes. A post from a real account is much harder to fake, and it tells your friends about the crabs.


One wallet per person?
One wallet per X account. Accounts that were made yesterday to farm spots get removed by hand before the mint.


Can people see which wallet is mine?
Yes. The code on your card is made from your wallet address, so the post links your X account to that wallet for anyone who checks. Use a wallet you are happy to have linked to your X account. 


What does the mint cost, and how many crabs are there?
2,222 crabs, free to mint, on Robinhood Chain.


Do I need $CRAB to get on the list?
No. $CRAB does not exist yet. It launches after the mint, and you only need it then, to wake your crab and level its shell. How the token works. How to spot the real one on launch day.


Will you message me?
No, never. We do not send direct messages, and we never ask for a seed phrase, a signature or a payment to be on the list. Anyone who does is not us.
