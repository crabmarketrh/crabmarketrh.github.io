    How it works

    A crab, a shell, an island, a tide.

    Four steps from mint to pay. The pay is USDG, a digital dollar, bought with the fees from $CRAB trading.

    Step 1

    Mint a crab

    Minting is free: you pay only the network fee. Your crab arrives asleep. Token number 1 is picture number 1, forever: you see your crab the moment you mint it.

    Step 2

    Wake it up

    Burn some $CRAB to wake your crab. It climbs into its shell, its island turns from grey to colour, and it starts earning.

    Step 3

    Level up the shell

    Five levels. Each one burns more $CRAB, gives the crab a bigger share of the pay, and grows its island. The level shows on this site, on the island and as pips over the crab.

    Step 4

    The tide pays

    Every hour the pot buys USDG (digital dollars) and credits it to each awake crab's tide pool. Claim it and it is paid straight to you.

    The tide

    Every hour, on the hour, the crabs get paid.

    • All hour long, people trade $CRAB and a share of every trade fills the pot.
    • At :00 the pot buys USDG (digital dollars).
    • Each awake crab's share drops into its tide pool, where it waits to be claimed.

    The token is not live yet, so today the clock only shows the rhythm. If nobody trades in an hour, that hour pays nothing.

    Questions

    Short answers.

    Is anything live yet?

    No. There is no mint and no token today. The list is the first thing to open. If you see a token with our name before we announce it here, it is fake.

    What does the mint cost?

    Nothing: the mint is free. You pay only the network fee, which on Robinhood Chain is a fraction of a cent.

    Where does the hourly pay really come from?

    From trading fees on $CRAB. A fixed share of those fees goes into a pot. Once an hour the pot buys USDG (digital dollars) and hands them to the awake crabs.

    If trading is quiet the pot is small. If nobody trades, that hour pays nothing.

    Where does my pay actually go?

    Into your crab's tide pool, where it waits until you claim it. Claiming pays you directly. While it waits, another crab can raid the pool and take half of what is in it, so claim it when it is worth claiming. If you sell the crab, whatever is in the pool settles to you rather than to the buyer. How raiding works.

    Which crab will I get?

    The one with your token number: crab number 1 is picture number 1, forever, and you see it the moment you mint. The whole collection, legends included, is published before the mint opens at an address the contract locks and nobody can change, so anyone can look through it and time a legend at the counter. We chose the simplest reveal knowing that. Nothing about the picture ever changes; the level, the items and the pool are shown on this site.

    Do I get paid for holding $CRAB?

    No. Only awake crabs get paid. $CRAB is what wakes them, levels them up, and fills the pot when it is traded. More token questions are answered here.

    What are island items?

    Things you buy for your crab's island by burning $CRAB: a hot tub, a jukebox, a helipad. Each one raises the crab's share of the hourly pot, and it stays with the crab forever. A crab has one item slot per shell level, and items can never add more than 100% to its level weight. They give you a bigger slice, they do not make the pot bigger. Try them in the shop.

    Do I need $CRAB to mint?

    No. You mint with ETH. You only need $CRAB later, to wake your crab and level its shell.

    What happens when I sell a crab?

    It goes back to sleep, and the buyer has to wake it again. Anything still in its pool settles to you, the seller, so you keep what it earned and there is nothing to empty first. The $CRAB you burned does not come back. Its shell level is permanent: the buyer only has to wake it again, and the level shows on the OpenSea listing.

    Can I own more than one?

    Yes. Each crab keeps its own island and its own level. Your page shows all your islands together in one sea.
