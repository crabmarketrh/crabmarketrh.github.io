The raid feed

Every raid, on the record too.

Pay waits in a crab's tide pool until its owner claims it. While it waits, any other awake crab can raid that pool and take 50% of what is in it. Every raid that ever happens is listed here, both sides named. The pools page explains the rules and lets you try a raid.

Play a raid

Claiming is the defence. An empty pool cannot be raided, and a pool holding less than 5 USDG cannot be raided at all. A pool can only be hit once every 12 hours, and a crab can only raid once every 12 hours. Every raid burns a fixed 10,000 $CRAB, which goes to nobody.

How to check a tide yourself.

1

Open the transaction

Every row links to the transaction on the chain's public explorer. It shows how much USDG the pot bought and which crabs were paid.

2

Check the contract

The contract that runs the tide is not deployed yet. Its address will be listed here and in the docs, and nowhere else first.

3

Look inside a pool

A tide pool is not a wallet. It is what the tide contract owes that crab, and it is visible on the explorer: each tide arriving, each raid leaving, and what is still waiting to be claimed.

Pay comes only from trading fees on $CRAB. It rises and falls with trading, and it can be zero. It also waits in your crab's tide pool until you claim it, and a raid can take 50% of what is waiting. Try the pay simulator or read the risks.
