The honest line: items give your crab a bigger slice. They do not make the pot bigger. When other crabs buy items, your slice gets smaller again. No trading still means no pay.

How items work

Four rules, all in the contract.

1

Burned, all of it

The whole price is burned. None of it goes to the team, and none goes back into the pot. The supply of $CRAB gets smaller for good.

2

One slot per level

A level 1 crab holds one item, a level 5 crab holds five. One of each item per crab. A sleeping crab cannot buy.

3

A hard cap

Items can add at most 100% to a crab's level weight. A purchase that would go past the cap is refused, so nobody burns tokens for a bonus that does not count. Levelling up raises the cap.

4

Part of the crab

An item is not a separate NFT. It stays on the island forever, and if you sell the crab the items go with it.
