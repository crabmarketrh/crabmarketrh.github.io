Who can you raid

The board is public.

Every pool that is worth raiding is on one list, biggest first, for everyone to see. If your pool is up there, so is everyone else's chance to take half of it.

At launch your own crabs sit at the top of this board, so you can see what you are leaving out and claim it in one tap.

The rules

Six things to know.

1

Only unclaimed pay

A raid can only take what is still sitting in the pool. Claim it and it is yours for good. Your shell level, your items and your crab itself can never be taken.

2

Raiding burns $CRAB

Every raid burns 10,000 $CRAB, gone for good. Nobody is paid that fee. It also means a small pool is never worth raiding.

3

Small pools are safe

A pool holding less than 5 USDG cannot be raided at all, and the same pool can only be hit once every 12 hours.

4

You need an awake crab

You raid with a crab of your own, and it must be awake. A sleeping crab can neither raid nor be raided, because it has no pool.

5

Some items guard you

A few things in the shop take points off any raid against you: the observatory, the golden anchor and the kraken. They use a slot like any other item, so guarding your pool costs you a slot that could have earned more. See them in the shop.

6

Ten crabs are untouchable

Guarding items only shave points: together they take at most 25 off, so the worst a raid can be reduced to is 25% of the pool. The one exception is the sea fort. Only 10 will ever exist, and a crab holding one cannot be raided at all. For every other crab, claiming is still the only way to lose nothing. See the fort in the shop.

This is the part of the project that can cost you money. Pay that you have not claimed is not safe. If that is not for you, claim often, or leave your crab asleep and it is out of the game entirely.

Raiding is not switched on yet. It starts a couple of days after launch, once the hourly rounds have run cleanly. Until then every pool is safe. Every raid will be listed here.
