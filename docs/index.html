What it is

Crab Market is a collection of 2,222 pixel hermit crabs on Robinhood Chain, and a token, $CRAB.

People trade the token. A share of the trading fees fills a pot. Once an hour the pot buys USDG (digital dollars) and credits each awake crab's tide pool. That is the whole machine.

It is run by a small anonymous team. You should judge it by the code and the addresses, which will all be public, not by who we say we are.

The mint

  • Price: free, plus the network fee.
  • Supply: 2,222, including 10 one-of-a-kind legends.
  • The list mints first. Get on it here.
  • At most 2 crabs per wallet in total, whichever round you mint in.
  • The team mints 50 crabs to its own treasury before the public round, for giveaways and partners, in a round of its own that closes before the list round opens: they take the first numbers, so no legend is among them. Woken, they are paid like any other crab.
  • Minting ends when the collection sells out or 24 hours after the public round opens, whichever comes first. After that the supply is fixed at whatever was minted, forever.
  • The date is not set yet. It will be announced here and on our X account, and nowhere else first.

You see your crab the moment you mint it. Every picture is published before the mint opens, in token order, at an address the contract locks, and there is no shuffle afterwards: token 1 is picture 1, forever. That also means the whole collection, legends included, is public before the mint, so anyone can see which numbers the legends are and try to time their mint for them. We chose the simplest reveal knowing that, and we would rather say so here than pretend the order is a secret. The ten legends sit at ten fixed numbers spread through the public mint, one in each tenth of it and never among the team's crabs: . A mint is sequential, so a legend goes to whoever happens to mint its number; if the mint seals before a number is reached, that legend is never minted.

The token

$CRAB is for using. It does three jobs:

  • You burn it to wake a crab.
  • You burn more of it for each shell level.
  • Every trade of it pays a small fee, and a fixed share of that fee fills the hourly pot.

Holding $CRAB pays nothing. Only awake crabs get paid.

It will launch on Pons, on Robinhood Chain, after the mint. It does not exist today, so anything sold under that name now is fake.

There is no presale and no private round. The team makes one opening buy on the open market when the token launches, like any other buyer. Its size and the wallet will be published here before launch.

Waking and levels

A new crab is asleep beside its empty shell, and a sleeping crab earns nothing. To wake it you burn $CRAB. Burned tokens are destroyed for good.

After that there are 5 shell levels. Each level costs a bigger burn and gives the crab a bigger share of every hourly pot. The level shows on this site: it grows the crab's island and its share, and it is what the shop and the tide pools read.

LevelBurnShare weight

If you sell or transfer a crab it goes back to sleep, and the new owner has to wake it again. Whether the shell level survives a sale is not decided yet, and this page will say so before launch. The $CRAB you burned never comes back, whether you keep the crab or sell it. What the crab already earned settles to you when you sell.

The level does not change the NFT itself. The picture and the traits a marketplace shows are fixed at the mint and never change, so an OpenSea listing looks the same at level 5 as at level 0. To see a crab's level, its items and its pool before you buy one, look it up here.

Island items

An awake crab can buy items for its island: a hot tub, a jukebox, a water slide, a helipad. You pay by burning $CRAB, the same way you pay for levels, and each item adds a little to the crab's share weight. See them all in the shop.

  • The whole price is burned. Nothing from an item goes to the team.
  • A crab has one item slot per shell level, and can own one of each item.
  • All its items together can add at most 100% of its level weight. That limit will be fixed in the contract and nobody can raise it. A purchase that would pass it is refused, and levelling up raises it.
  • Items stay with the crab forever. If you sell the crab, they go with it, like its level. While the crab is asleep after a sale they stay on the island and count for nothing, until the new owner wakes it.
  • A few items are limited editions. When they are gone, they are gone.

Items give your crab a bigger slice. They do not make the pot bigger. When other crabs buy items, your slice gets smaller again.

The hourly tide

StepWhat happens
1A share of every $CRAB trade is kept as a fee.
2The fee is split between the hourly pot and the team. The split is fixed in the contract and anyone can read it.
3Once an hour, the pot buys USDG (digital dollars) on the open market.
4The pot is split by shell level. Each level has a share weight, and each awake crab's share drops into its tide pool.

Anyone will be able to press the button that runs the hourly round, so it does not depend on the team being online. We will also run a bot that does it. "Hourly" is the target: if the bot is late, the round runs late and the pot simply carries over. A round whose purchase cannot fill (a thin market, or a paused dollar) carries over too, and a carried pot is shared by whoever is awake at the round that does fill. One round buys at most 50 ETH worth; a bigger pot is bought over several rounds.

The tide pools

Pay does not go straight to you. It collects in the tide pool on your crab's island, and it stays there until you claim it. While it is sitting there, any other awake crab can raid the pool and take 50% of what is in it. Watch it happen here.

  • Claiming is the defence. Once the pay is out of the pool it cannot be touched. Only the sea fort, below, protects a pool that is left sitting.
  • Every raid burns 10,000 $CRAB. Nobody is paid that fee, so a small pool is never worth raiding.
  • The same pool can only be raided once every 12 hours, and a pool holding less than 5 USDG cannot be raided at all.
  • You raid with a crab of your own and it must be awake. A sleeping crab can neither raid nor be raided.
  • Your crab, its shell level and its items can never be taken. Only unclaimed pay is at risk.
  • A few items guard you. The observatory, the golden anchor and the kraken each take points off any raid against your pool. Together they can take off at most 25, so a raid can never be reduced below 25% of the pool. They use an item slot like anything else, so guarding costs you a slot that could have earned more.
  • One item is different. The sea fort, of which only 10 will ever exist, makes a crab impossible to raid at all rather than shaving points off. It is the only immunity in the game, it costs a slot like anything else, and it goes with the crab if the crab is sold.

Raiding is not switched on at launch. It starts a couple of days after launch, once the hourly rounds have run cleanly. Turning it on is a single call that the team can make once, and it can never be turned back off. That call cannot move anyone's money, and it is the only power the team has over this game.

Islands

Each crab has an island that is built from the crab's own number, so no two are alike and yours never changes shape on you. A sleeping crab's island is grey. Waking it brings the colour back, and each level adds land and builds something: a sandcastle, an umbrella, a palm and a hut, a dock with a boat, and at the top a lighthouse and a sand palace. If you own several crabs, your page shows all their islands in one sea.

The art

Every crab is 32 by 32 pixels, drawn from layers: 33 shells, body colours, eyes, mouths, claws, markings, accessories and backdrops. The finished picture and description of every crab are published on IPFS, addressed by their content, and the contract locks that address before the first mint: no key, ours included, can move it afterwards. We pin the files on more than one service, and because the address is the content, anyone who keeps a copy keeps every crab reachable. The picture is fixed forever: it does not change with the level or the items.

The generator throws away any crab that looks too much like one that already exists, so there are no near-twins in the collection.

What the team earns

  • The part of the trading fee that does not go to the hourly pot. The exact split will be published here before launch.
  • The mint: up to 2,222 crabs, free to mint, and the 50 team crabs.
  • Whether resales carry a royalty is not decided yet. It will be stated here before the mint.

That is all of it. Pons also keeps its own protocol cut of every trade, which never reaches us or the pot.

The list

The list mints before the public. You get on it by posting your card on X. Joining costs nothing beyond the post, and we never ask you to connect a wallet. The mint itself is free too. Make your card.

Risks, said plainly

  • Pay can be zero. It comes only from trading fees. Quiet market, small pot. No trading, no pay.
  • The token can fall. Waking and levelling burn tokens you cannot get back.
  • More awake crabs, smaller slices. The pot is shared. Every crab that wakes, levels up or buys an item makes every other share a little smaller.
  • USDG is someone else's dollar. It is a stablecoin run by a company, not by us. It is built to hold one dollar, but no stablecoin is free of risk, and its issuer can pause it or freeze an address. While it cannot move, claiming still works: the pay is set aside as a credit that no raid can reach, to withdraw once it moves again. If the issuer ever froze the tide contract itself, every unclaimed pool would be stuck for as long as the freeze lasted, and a wipe of a frozen address would destroy them. Swaps cost something, so the pot always buys slightly less than it holds.
  • Item burns cannot be undone, and other owners' items shrink your share.
  • Unclaimed pay can be taken. Once raiding is on, pay left sitting in your tide pool can be raided by another crab. Claiming is how you protect it.
  • The team keeps the collection's admin key. It holds the OpenSea settings and the royalty list, the same way our last two collections still do. It cannot touch the pay, the tide pools, the raids or anyone's crab. The only power over the game itself is the one-time raid switch above.
  • Smart contracts can have bugs. The code will be public and verified. That lowers the risk, it does not remove it.
  • The launchpad has owner powers. The token launches on Pons, whose owner can redirect a creator's fee address after a three-day public delay. If that ever happened, the pot would stop filling. We will say so on this page the moment it is announced.
  • This is a collectible, not an investment product, and nothing here is financial advice.
