Why trade it

$CRAB is for using, not for sitting on.

Three reasons people will need it, all about use and none about price.

Use 1

Every crab arrives asleep

All 2,222 crabs start asleep, and a sleeping crab earns nothing. The only way to wake one is to burn $CRAB. A crab that is sold or moved goes back to sleep, and the new owner has to wake it again. Whether the shell level survives a sale is not decided yet. The $CRAB you burned never comes back.

Use 2

5 levels, each a bigger burn

Each level gives a crab a bigger share of every hourly pot, and each one costs more $CRAB than the last. Burned tokens do not go to the team and are not recycled. They are gone for good.

Use 3

Every trade feeds the crabs

Each buy and each sell pays the fee that fills the pot, whoever makes the trade. A busy market means a bigger tide. You can watch it happen on the chain, hour by hour.

Questions

Short answers about $CRAB.

Do I get paid for holding $CRAB?

No. Only awake crabs get paid. The token is what you burn to wake a crab and to level it up.

Do I need $CRAB to mint a crab?

No. The mint is free. You need $CRAB afterwards, to wake the crab.

Do I need a crab to trade $CRAB?

No. Anyone can trade it once it is live. Every trade feeds the pot, whoever makes it.

Where does the rest of the fee go?

To the team and to the launchpad. The split is fixed in the contract and anyone can read it.

What happens to burned tokens?

They are destroyed. They do not go to the team, to a treasury or back into the pot. The supply gets smaller by that amount, for good.

What if nobody trades?

Then the pot is empty and that hour pays nothing. Pay rises and falls with trading volume, and it can be zero.

What are island items?

A second use for $CRAB. You burn it to put an item on your crab's island, for good, and the item gives the crab a bigger share of every hourly pot. The whole price is burned. Items give your crab a bigger slice. They do not make the pot bigger. See the island shop.

Is my pay safe once my crab earns it?

Not until you claim it. Pay collects in your crab's tide pool. While it waits there, any other awake crab can raid the pool and take 50% of what is in it, once every 12 hours. Claiming empties the pool, so claiming is your defence (only the ten crabs holding a sea fort cannot be raided at all), and once it is claimed it is in your own wallet and yours for good. Selling the crab does not hand the pool over either: it settles to whoever owned it at the time. A raid costs a fixed 10,000 $CRAB, burned, so small pools are not worth raiding. Try the raid toy, and every raid that happens is listed here.

What is USDG, and is it safe?

It is what the crabs get paid in: a digital dollar. USDG is someone else's dollar. It is a stablecoin run by a company, not by us. It is built to hold one dollar, but no stablecoin is free of risk.

Can the pot stop filling?

Yes, in two ways. If nobody trades, there is no fee. And the token launches on Pons, whose owner has some powers over where a creator's fee is sent. The risks section of the docs explains this in full.

Will the price go up because of the burns?

Nobody can promise that, and we do not. Burns make the supply smaller. The price is set by buyers and sellers, and it can fall.
