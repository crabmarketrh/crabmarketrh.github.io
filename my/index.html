    How it works

    Three things you can do here.

    Wake

    Burn 30,000 $CRAB

    An asleep crab earns nothing. Waking it burns the tokens for good and it earns from the next tide. The first wake also sets level 1. A crab that was sold goes back to sleep, keeps its level, and needs waking again.

    Level up

    Climb the ladder

    Levels 2 to 5 burn more and earn a bigger share of every hourly pot. The ladder is cumulative: a climb costs the difference, and levels are permanent.

    Claim

    Take your tide pools

    Pay collects in each crab's tide pool until you claim it. Claim one crab, or every pool in one go. If the pay token cannot move at that moment it is banked for you, and a withdraw button appears.

    Each burn button shows the amount it burns. A burn asks the wallet twice: first an approval for exactly that amount of $CRAB, then the action itself. This page never asks for anything else. Every action is rehearsed against the chain before it is sent, so a move that would fail is refused for free.
